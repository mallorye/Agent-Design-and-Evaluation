## Rules the schema doesn't state

**designation**
- `areaofgiving` (where the gift goes, by category): use these core values: Revenue, Unrestricted Operations, Restricted Program Support, Student Financial Aid, Faculty & Staff Support, Research, Athletics, Capital - Endowment, Capital - Facilities, plus Unassigned for the holding account only. The schema deliberately has no CHECK on this column; don't invent other values.
- `desgtype` (the limitation on the gift): keep the values the previous run used, plus Unassigned for the holding account only. Gift-Non Deductible marks goods-and-services money.
- The two columns pair plausibly only (e.g. Capital - Endowment with Endowment, Capital - Facilities with Property Buildings Equipment). The CRM assigns them, so contradictory pairs don't occur.
- Every `desgtype` value has designations with gifts in them, including Gift-Non Deductible.
- The holding account designation (01009900, "Gift Holding Account") stays, with `desgtype` = Unassigned and `areaofgiving` = Unassigned. No other designation uses Unassigned.

**contribution**
- `contribution_number` is shared by every row of one contribution, across hard and soft credits and all designations.
- Rows sharing a contribution number share date, contribution type, payment type, pledge status, and anonymity.
- Payment type appears on gifts and payment rows, and also on Pledge and Matching Gift rows: the method recorded when the commitment was made. Their payments may later use a different method. Bequest Expectancy rows have none.
- Split gifts: each designation row carries its portion. The hard-credit rows of a contribution sum to its total.
- Soft-credit rows mirror the hard-credit row for the same designation: same amount.
- `pledge_number`: required on Pledge Payment rows, where it equals the original pledge's contribution number. On Pledge rows it is blank or equal to the row's own contribution number. Blank on all other rows.
- Amount paid and balance: on Pledge rows only, soft-credit rows included (shadow balances).
- Open pledges: paid + balance = amount, balance > 0. Paid pledges: balance 0. Canceled and Written Off: amount paid stays as received; balance is 0.
- Amount paid on each pledge designation = sum of payments with that pledge number and designation, dated on or before September 23, 2026. Payments are dated after their pledge.

**degree**
- One row per degree per constituent. Minors are not stored.
- `degree_awarded`: your choice of standard degree codes, plus ND for no degree.
- ND is rare and only for people who attended before 1980. The typical case is the spouse of a donor who attended in the 1960s or 70s and didn't finish. ND rows still carry a `graduation_date` (before 1980): the commencement of the class they attended with.
- `graduation_year`: the calendar year of `graduation_date`.
- `college`: deliberately inconsistent. Conventions shift decade to decade: full names, abbreviations, former names, department or program names in place of a school, blanks. `division` is the clean field; `college` is the one people reach for only when nothing else segments a degree.
- `division` uses current names, from the same set as designation divisions.
- Rename: your choice of a division renamed on a date inside the history window. All its degree rows carry the new name in `division`; the old name may show up in `college`.
- Graduation dates fall before September 23, 2026, and before any death date.
- Degree rows and the Alumnus/Alumna affiliation agree. ND counts as alumni.

**constituent_relationship**
- Relationships are current: no former spouses or ended relationships.

## Variation

Every value allowed by a CHECK constraint appears in the data. In addition:

- Skewed giving: most constituents give little or nothing; a few give most of the dollars.
- Pledges in every status, with payments spread across fiscal years.
- Gifts split across multiple designations.
- Soft credits.
- Organization donors: corporations, foundations, matching-gift companies.
- Anonymous gifts.
- Gifts to every designation type.
- Deceased constituents, some with a death date and some without.
- Incomplete addresses.
- Contact preferences: university-wide and department-level; open-ended, expired, and future end dates.
- Relationships beyond spouse (parent/child, grandparent/grandchild, sibling).
- People with multiple degrees of different types and graduation dates.
