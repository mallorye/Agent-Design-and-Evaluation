Maintained By:  Data Team Analysts
Fiscal Year: Starts July 1 of Fiscal Year -1 and ends June 30th of Fiscal Year.
Database time range: No contribution data before start of fiscal year 2023
Contactable: A constituent is considered contactable if they are a) living b) have at least one method of contact populated � address, email, phone c) do not have a do not communication, solicitation or event invite exclusion across every method of contact available. An exclusion applies only within its scope (see Contact Preference): someone may be non-contactable for a department but contactable university-wide.
Contact Preference: Three types: communication, solicitation and events.  A do not contact , do not invite to event or do not solicit exclusion applies to department. If the department is null, the exclusion applies University�Wide.
Constituent: status= active individual not an organization. Use constituent entity_type (P or C)
FY Donor: Any hard or soft credit gift, any contribution type given within a fiscal year. 
DAF or Donor Advised Fund: These are represented as entities where constituent.primary_affiliation = �Foundation�
Parent: Personal relationship is Parent. May have other affiliations.
Householding logic: The primary spouse is person1 in the constituent_relationship table where person1_role is Spouse. If an individual is single, they do not have an entry on this table and are considered the primary spouse. 
Hard credit donor: the entity that provides the funds and receives a tax receipt.
Soft credit donor: Recognizes individual(s) who made gift possible without legally owning or donating the money. Could be spouse or couple on a DAF gift.
Pledge: A promise to pay in planned installments over time. In contribution_type as �Pledge.�
Pledge Payment: Payment on a pledge. In our system will be linked by the pledge_number. In contribution_type as �Pledge Payment.�
Matching Gift: An amount an entity has agreed to pay in response to an eligible gift from a donor. Hard credit to entity that will pay, soft credit to donor.
Matching Gift Payment: Payment in response to matching gift, hard credit to the paying entity, soft credit to the original donor who set up the matching gift.
Gift: contribution_type = Gift, is a straightforward gift 
Bequest  Expectancy = Promised money from a donor to pay upon passing away. Drawn up in an agreement.
Bequest Expectancy Payment = pay out of what was promised in Bequest Expectancy
Commitment Fundraising Definition = Theme: what was promised. Gifts with contribution type: Pledge, Gift, Matching Gift, Bequest Expectancy. If Pledge = canceled, substitute pledge_amount_paid instead of amount.(see contribution.pledge_status) Hard credit only for university, unit, or group totals, both hard and soft credit for individual totals and amounts.
Cash Fundraising Definition = cash in hand. Gifts with contribution type Pledge Payment, Gift, Matching Gift Payment, Bequest Expectancy Payment. Hard credit only for university, unit, or group totals, both hard and soft credit for individual totals and amounts.
Definition of gift to University = What was given to one contribution number � may represent gifts to multiple designations.
Definition of gift to Department or Unit = part of the gift that maps to the designation and related division.
Today�s date is always Sept 23, 2026

Ambiguity Blocks:
year_basis:
Applies when a request refers to �last/this year� or �last X years� or names a year without specifying whether it is calendar or fiscal. 
Interpretations: fiscal year (July 1�June 30; see Fiscal Year entry) | calendar year (Jan 1�Dec 31)
Default: fiscal year
credit_scope:
Applies when a list of donors is asked for or a financial total without saying how credit is counted.
Interpretations: hard credit only (legal donors; see Hard Credit entry) | hard + soft credit (includes recognized individuals, e.g. spouses, matched donors; see Soft Credit entry)
Default: None � must ask
counting_basis:
Applies when: a request asks for a fundraising total, amount raised, or money brought in without naming a basis.
Interpretations: commitment totals (see Commitment Fundraising Definition) |Cash (see Cash Fundraising Definition.)
Default: Commitment Fundraising Definition
contactable_scope
 Applies when: a request asks for the contactable base, or for a list of people to contact, solicit, invite, or mail, without stating the contact purpose.
 Interpretations: solicitation (DNS and DNC exclusions both exclude) | non-solicitation outreach (only DNC excludes)
 Default: None � must ask
entity_scope
 Applies when: a contactability request names no department � �how many people can we email?�, �pull a mailing list� � leaving open whose exclusions govern.
 Interpretations: university-wide (unscoped exclusions only; see Contact Preference entry) | department-scoped (a named unit�s exclusions layered on the university�s exclusion)
 Default: university-wide unless specified.
